Treatment & Prevention Guide

Integrated pest management strategies combining biological, cultural, and chemical controls

Prevention Strategies

🌱

Crop Rotation

Strategic rotation breaks disease cycles and reduces pathogen buildup in soil.

Breaks disease cycles Improves soil health Reduces pesticide needs
🌿

Resistant Varieties

Planting disease-resistant crop varieties reduces infection risk by 60-80%.

Natural resistance Reduced treatments Higher yields
💧

Water Management

Proper irrigation timing and drainage prevents fungal diseases and root rot.

Reduces humidity Prevents overwatering Improves air circulation

Parasitic Wasps Control caterpillars

Microbes that suppress plant pathogens and enhance plant immunity.

Cultural Control Practices

Sanitation Practices

  • Remove infected plant debris immediately
  • Disinfect tools between plants
  • Control weeds that harbor pests
  • Maintain clean greenhouse environments

Timing & Spacing

  • Plant at optimal spacing for air circulation
  • Time planting to avoid peak disease periods
  • Harvest crops at proper maturity
  • Rotate planting locations annually
