Irrigation Technologies

Advanced irrigation systems designed for maximum water efficiency in arid environments

💧

Drip Irrigation

⭐ 95% Efficiency
PREMIUM

Precise water delivery directly to plant roots through a network of tubes, pipes, and emitters. The gold standard for water conservation in Jordan's agriculture.

Key Benefits

  • Reduces water usage by 30-50%
  • Minimizes soil erosion and nutrient loss
  • Enables fertigation (fertilizer delivery)
  • Reduces weed growth and disease

Micro-Sprinklers
