Agriculture by Region

Each region of Jordan has unique agricultural characteristics and specializations

Jordan Valley

The most fertile and productive agricultural region, benefiting from the Jordan River and favorable climate conditions.

45% Of national production
28,000 Hectares cultivated
85% Irrigation efficiency

Primary Crops

Tomatoes Cucumbers Bananas Citrus Peppers Dates

Northern & Central Highlands

Cooler climate and higher elevation suitable for cereals, olives, and stone fruits. Rain-fed agriculture predominates.

35% Of national production
75,000 Hectares cultivated
65% Rain-fed farming

Primary Crops

Wheat Barley Olives Stone Fruits Lentils Chickpeas

Eastern Desert

Arid region with limited rainfall, focusing on drought-resistant crops and innovative irrigation techniques.

12% Of national production
35,000 Hectares cultivated
95% Drip irrigation

Primary Crops

Durum Wheat Desert Dates Desert Vegetables Medicinal Plants Sorghum

Southern Regions

Hot, arid climate with innovative greenhouse systems and specialized desert agriculture techniques.

8% Of national production
18,000 Hectares cultivated
75% Greenhouse farming

Primary Crops

Greenhouse Tomatoes Peppers Melons Herbs Desert Fruits
